**Status:** PENDING · **Date opened:** 2026-06-11 · **Due:** 2026-06-19 · **Deciders:** Odei, supervisors

## Context

The outdoor generality track is Synth4D→SemanticKITTI and Synth4D→nuScenes per the GIPSO/HGL
protocol with a new PTv3 teacher
([memo §5](../HINTpp_Design_Memo_R1_2026-06-11.md)). The source dataset must be fixed before the
outdoor teacher training is committed; descope checkpoints are Jul 24 and Aug 7.

## Options

1. **Synth4D** — matches the GIPSO/HGL protocol most directly; numbers are comparable to published
   baselines with no protocol translation.
2. **SynLiDAR** — larger scale and richer label diversity; weaker precedent in the GIPSO/HGL
   protocol, so baseline comparability must be re-established.
